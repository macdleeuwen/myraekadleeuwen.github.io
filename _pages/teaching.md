I have been instructor of record for several classes at UNC, and have TAed for a number of classes at both UNC and Northeastern. Below, you can find links to abridged versions of the syllabi for courses at UNC for which I have been instructor of record.

[Gateway to Politics, Philosophy, and Economics]({{ '/assets/pdf/abridged384syllabus.pdf' | relative_url }})
  - An interdisciplinary upper/middle level course which introduces topics and tools used to analyze issues in PPE. 
  
[Ethics of Peace, War, and Defense]({{ '/assets/pdf/abridged272syllabus.pdf' | relative_url }})
  - An introductory class on the ethics of war. 

[AI and the Future of Humanity]({{ '/assets/pdf/abridged143syllabus.pdf' | relative_url }})
  - An introductory course on topics related to technology in society (e.g., privacy, information online, AI ethics, and conscious systems). 

[Moral and Philosophical Issues of Gender in Society]({{ '/assets/pdf/abridged275syllabus.pdf' | relative_url }})
  - An introductory course on topics in social metaphysics and feminist philosophy.
